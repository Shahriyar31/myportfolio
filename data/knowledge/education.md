## M.Sc. Data Science at TUHH
Since October 2023 I have been studying for an M.Sc. in Data Science at Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH) in Hamburg, Germany, covering machine learning, big data, statistics and digital twins, alongside my research and my job at Nordex.

## B.Tech. Computer Science at CGEC
From July 2018 to August 2022 I studied for a B.Tech. in Computer Science at Cooch Behar Government Engineering College in West Bengal, India. I graduated in the top 10% with a CGPA of 8.73 out of 10, and served as a teaching assistant and a student council member. Core courses included algorithms, data structures, operating systems, DBMS, software engineering, discrete mathematics and networks.

## The year in between
Between my bachelor's and master's (2022–2023) I prepared for Germany at home in West Bengal: university applications, my admission to TUHH for the M.Sc. Data Science, finances and paperwork, and my student visa. Then I moved to Hamburg in 2023.
