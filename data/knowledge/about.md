## Who I am
I'm Farhan Shahriyar, an AI Engineer based in Hamburg, Germany. I build secure, governed AI on Azure: RAG systems and agents, AI governance (EU AI Act, GDPR, NIST AI RMF) and AI security (OWASP LLM Top 10, DevSecOps). I work as a working student in Enterprise Data Management & AI at Nordex Group and study for an M.Sc. in Data Science at Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH).

## What I do, in three parts
First, I build AI that knows a business: assistants and agents that answer from an organisation's own documents, with sources you can check (Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, RAG, LangGraph). Second, I keep AI legal and trusted: I work out what the EU AI Act and GDPR require and build it into the product (EU AI Act, GDPR, NIST AI RMF, OWASP LLM Top 10). Third, I ship AI safely: secure pipelines with no leaked keys, no vulnerable parts and no data left open (Azure DevOps, Docker, Syft, Grype, Cosign).

## What makes me different
I can take an AI idea to a working product, I make it compliant and secure from the start rather than as an afterthought, and I can explain it clearly to non-technical people. I have also coordinated cross-team delivery, so I'm comfortable owning a piece of work end to end.
