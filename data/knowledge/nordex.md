## My role at Nordex Group
Since August 2025 I have been a Working Student (Werkstudent) in Enterprise Data Management & AI at Nordex Group in Hamburg, a global wind-turbine manufacturer. I'm part of the enterprise data and AI team, working where AI governance, AI security and applied AI on Azure meet. My work spans four areas: AI governance policy, AI security analysis, an AI knowledge agent, and AI project management.

## AI governance policy (Aug 2025 – now)
I authored the AI lifecycle framework for the company's AI governance policy: approval gates from idea to retirement, clear roles, and what happens when a model changes or something goes wrong. It is aligned with GDPR, the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF. I also wrote the LLM security and guardrails part: prompt injection, least-privilege tools and API controls.

## AI security analysis (Feb 2026 – now)
I mapped the OWASP LLM Top 10 to Azure-native mitigations for a RAG system, designed security controls for MCP tool access (delegated sign-in and confused-deputy prevention), compared supply-chain security tools (SBOMs, signing and scanning) and delivered a CI/CD security pipeline, aligned with EU AI Act Article 15, ISO 27001 and IEC 62443.

## AI knowledge agent (Jan – Mar 2026)
I built a RAG knowledge agent on Azure AI Foundry with hybrid vector search, custom function tools and a fast pre-routing layer, and ran an LLM-as-judge evaluation comparing Azure OpenAI models on quality, speed and cost to guide the model choice.

## AI project management (Feb 2026 – now)
I coordinate delivery across several internal and partner teams to unblock infrastructure work: weekly alignment, blocker and timeline tracking, meeting minutes, and network and API-gateway integration through to resolution.
